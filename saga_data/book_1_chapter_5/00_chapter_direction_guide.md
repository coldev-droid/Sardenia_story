## 4. Location Intelligence Dossier

### Primary Location A: Grotta di Nettuno & Capo Caccia Promontory
* **Geographic Coordinates**: 40°33'38" N, 8°09'53" E
* **Physical Description**: Marine karst cave located at the base of the 110-meter sheer limestone cliffs of Capo Caccia, 24 km west of Alghero.
* **Key Features**:
  * **Escala del Cabirol**: "Goat's Scale" stairwell cut into the cliff face (654 steps).
  * **Lago Lamarmora**: Underground saltwater lake, approx. 100 meters long, up to 9 meters deep, featuring a central stalagmite column formation known as the *Reggia* (Palace) or *Liofon* / *Tribuna della Musica*.
* **Source Verification**:
  * Source: *Sardegna Turismo / Ente Foreste della Sardegna*
  * URL: https://www.sardegnaturismo.it/en/explore/grotta-di-nettuno
  * Classification: `VERIFIED FACT`

### Primary Location B: Rada di Alghero (Gulf of Alghero) & Port Quay
* **Geographic Coordinates**: 40°33'26" N, 8°18'44" E
* **Physical Description**: Protected bay bounded by Capo Caccia to the west and Punta Giglio. The port sits beneath the yellow sandstone Aragonese ramparts (Bastione di San Giacomo, Bastione di Cristoforo Colombo).
