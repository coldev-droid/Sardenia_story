# 06_historical_people_and_artists.md
- **Grazia Deledda (1871–1936):** Nobel laureate writer. Documented works provide keys to the Erchitu and Panas myths.
- **Maria Lai (1919–2013):** Artist from Ulassai. "Legarsi alla montagna" event used as a blueprint for the loom puzzles.
- **Giovanni Lilliu (1914–2012):** Archaeologist who excavated Su Nuraxi. Field notes guide the team.
- **Costantino Nivola (1911–1988):** Sculptor from Orani. Sand-casting techniques mirror amulet forging.
- **Eleonora d'Arborea (1340-1404):** Judike of Arborea. Her *Carta de Logu* provides a historical cipher.
- **Amedeo Nazzari (1907-1979):** Actor, indirect reference to mid-century Sardinian cinema history.
- **Domenico Alberto Azuni (1749-1827):** Jurist; navigational laws used to solve a maritime puzzle in Book I.
