# 05_myth_and_legend_register.md

## 36 Candidates Researched, 31 Approved, 5 Rejected

**Classifications used:** `SUPERNATURAL_FOLK_BEING`, `FOLK_BELIEF_OR_OMEN`, `RITUAL_MASK_TRADITION`, `LOCAL_LEGEND`, `RELIGIOUS_OR_PROCESSIONAL_TRADITION`, `UNVERIFIED_CANDIDATE`

### 5 Potentially Helpful
1. **Janas** (SUPERNATURAL_FOLK_BEING) - Long-term ally. Small fairies associated with Domus de Janas, keepers of weaving and treasures.
2. **Issohadores** (RITUAL_MASK_TRADITION) - Lasso rescue mechanic (fictionalized application of ritual luck-catching).
3. **Sa Filonzana** (RITUAL_MASK_TRADITION) - Puzzle resolution for a price (fate weaver).
4. **Merdules** (RITUAL_MASK_TRADITION) - Animal control mechanisms (handlers of the Boes).
5. **Sos Tamburinos** (RITUAL_MASK_TRADITION) - Time/rhythm sync to break enchantments (Gavoi).

### 5 Morally Ambiguous
6. **Erchitu** (SUPERNATURAL_FOLK_BEING) - Tied to justice/guilt. White ox omen of death.
7. **Boes** (RITUAL_MASK_TRADITION) - Relentless, unthinking force.
8. **Thurpos** (RITUAL_MASK_TRADITION) - Blind seekers from Orotelli.
9. **Su Bundhu** (RITUAL_MASK_TRADITION) - Associated with wind/storms and agricultural cycles (Orani).
10. **Luxia Rabiosa** (LOCAL_LEGEND) - Petrified woman due to extreme greed/hubris.

### 5 Hunters, Thieves or Deceivers
11. **Cogas** (SUPERNATURAL_FOLK_BEING) - Hunter (Memory). Shapeshifting vampiric witches.
12. **S'Ammutadori** (SUPERNATURAL_FOLK_BEING) - Hunter (Sleep Paralysis). Chest pressure, nightmare feeder.
13. **Maskinganna** (SUPERNATURAL_FOLK_BEING) - Deceiver (Illusions). Tricks travelers in forests.
14. **Mommotti** (SUPERNATURAL_FOLK_BEING) - Hunter (Shadow stalker). Bogeyman entity.
15. **S'Orcu** (SUPERNATURAL_FOLK_BEING) - Hunter/Blocker. Giant/Ogre guarding bottlenecks.

### 3 Guardians
16. **Panas** (SUPERNATURAL_FOLK_BEING) - Guardian (Silence/Water). Spirits of women who died in childbirth, washing clothes at night.
17. **Su Scultone** (SUPERNATURAL_FOLK_BEING) - Guardian (Stasis/Stone). Dragon of the Golgo abyss.
18. **Sos Corriolos** (RITUAL_MASK_TRADITION) - Guardian (Bone rhythms/Deer horns) of Neoneli.

21. **Su Boe Muliache** (SUPERNATURAL_FOLK_BEING) - Opposes the Erchitu in territorial disputes.
