# 08_living_heritage_register.md
- **Canto a Tenore:** Polyphonic folk singing (Barbagia). UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage.
- **Launeddas:** Ancient triple-pipe instrument. Used in acoustic magic suppression.
- **Sartiglia:** Equestrian tournament in Oristano. Source of agility/combat lore.
- **Bisso Weaving:** Sant'Antioco. The silk of the sea.
- **Carnival Masks:** Mamoiada, Ottana, Orotelli (Treated strictly as ritual masks, not literal monsters).
- **Su ballu de s'arzia:** Spider possession dance.
- **Pane Pintau:** Ceremonial intricately carved bread.
