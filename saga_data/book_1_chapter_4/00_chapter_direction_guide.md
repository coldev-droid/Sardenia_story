In Chapter 4, Team A moves inside the subterranean karst system of **Grotta di Nettuno**, specifically entering the perimeter of **Lago Lamarmora** (one of Europe’s largest underground saltwater lakes). 

* **Conduit Mechanism:** The acoustic vibration of *S'Urtzu* is not a creature, but a subterranean hydraulic-acoustic resonance created by compressed air pockets in the karst channels connecting the Gulf of Alghero to deep-seated fault lines (*Janna* network).

## 4. LOCATION INTELLIGENCE DOSSIER

### Site 1: Grotta di Nettuno & Escala del Cabirol (Capo Caccia, Alghero)
* **Coordinates:** 40.5636° N, 8.1611° E
* **Physical Description:** A marine karst cave located at the base of the sheer 110-meter limestone cliffs of Capo Caccia. The interior features *Lago Lamarmora* (saltwater lake ~100m long), massive stalactites/stalagmites, and chambers such as the *Sala Grande* and *Reggia*. Accessible from the sea or via the *Escala del Cabirol* ("Goat's Staircase"), a 654-step staircase cut into the cliff face in 1954.
