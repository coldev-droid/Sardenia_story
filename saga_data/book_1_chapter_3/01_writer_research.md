## Pass A - Writer Research

**Location 1: Porto di Alghero**
- Claim: The physical marina and port of the old town of Alghero.
- Source: Alghero Turismo (https://www.algheroturismo.eu/en/)
- Status: VERIFIED FACT

**Location 2: Capo Caccia / Grotta di Nettuno**
- Claim: A massive limestone promontory protecting the Gulf of Alghero. The Grotta di Nettuno is a major karst cave system at the base of the cliffs, with its sea entrance sitting just about 1 meter above sea level. It is accessible by boat.
- Source: Wikipedia / Alghero Turismo (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neptune%27s_Grotto)
- Status: VERIFIED FACT

**Myth 1: S'Urtzu**
- Claim: A traditional Sardinian carnival figure, often representing a sacrificial victim or untamed nature, dressed in dark animal skins with heavy cowbells. Usually associated with chaotic behavior, attacking people, and death/rebirth cycles.
- Source: Contusu (https://contusu.it) / Janas (https://janass.eu)
- Status: FOLK TRADITION

**Myth 2: S'Urtzu Acoustic Resonance**
- Claim: S'Urtzu manifests not as a man in skins, but as an uncontrollable, violent acoustic resonance within the limestone caves of Capo Caccia that mimics the sound of heavy cowbells and causes severe spatial disorientation.
- Source: N/A
- Status: ORIGINAL_FICTIONAL_TRANSFORMATION
