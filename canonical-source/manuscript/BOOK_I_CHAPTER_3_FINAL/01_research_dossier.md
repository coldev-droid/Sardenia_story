## 1. Executive Summary & Narrative Scope

Chapter 3 covers the maritime approach of the research team vessel from the open waters of northwestern Sardinia toward the Gulf of Alghero and the sheer limestone cliffs of Capo Caccia. The chapter details navigation past the marine reserve of Isola Piana, coastal sea cave topography (specifically Grotta di Nettuno and Grotta di Nereo), atmospheric conditions driven by the *Maestrale* (Mistral) wind, and the transition into local coastal folklore.
