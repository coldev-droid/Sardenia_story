# Direction Guide: Chapter 4

**Previous Scene Continuity:** 
Chapter 3 concluded with Team A (Geronimo, Veerle, Katia) inside the Grotta di Nettuno, having witnessed an acoustic phenomenon (the *S'Urtzu* resonance) drain the underground lake to reveal a carved bronze-age archway. Team B (Maris, Inga, André) was at the Alghero port, securing the yacht *Sentina*. 

**Current State & Location:**
- **Time/Weather:** Early evening, transitioning to dusk. The air is cooling, carrying the scent of sea salt and dry earth.
- **Location:** The action moves from the Alghero port to the inland archaeological site of Anghelu Ruju.
- **Custody:** Team A has their scanning equipment. Team B has the logistical gear, medical kits, and the Land Rover.

**Heritage & Reality Anchor:**
- Anghelu Ruju Necropolis: Discovered in 1903 on the Sella&Mosca estate. Contains 38 domus de janas (fairy houses) carved into sandstone. Features red ochre burials and bull horn carvings.

**Myth & Fiction Boundary:**
- **Myth:** The Janas (fairies/witches). 
- **Original Transformation:** The Janas are not tiny winged women; they are sonic, resonant entities—echoes of the matriarchal dead trapped in the sandstone, activated by the red ochre and the specific frequencies transmitted from Capo Caccia.
- **Mineral:** Red Ochre.

**Narrative Goal:**
Reunite the teams, explore the necropolis under failing light, trigger the Janas through acoustic feedback, and secure the Red Ochre amulet at the cost of a painful memory extraction.
