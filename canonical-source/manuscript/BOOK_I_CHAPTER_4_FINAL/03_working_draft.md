The architecture of the living, transplanted into the realm of the dead.

*Clang... clang...*

Geronimo knelt down, burying his hands in the thick fur of their necks. He looked at the sealed pouch containing the Red Ochre amulet, and then at the dark, silent coastline stretching out to the north. 

Two amulets secured. Ten remained. And the island was only just beginning to wake.

"Maris," Geronimo said, standing up. "Chart a course north. Get us out of this bay before sunrise. We need open water."

Maris nodded, moving immediately toward the helm. The engines of the *Sentina* rumbled to life, a deep, mechanical growl that chased away the lingering echoes of the ancient bells.
