# Research Dossier: Chapter 4

## 1. Location Data
- **Name:** Necropoli di Anghelu Ruju
- **Municipality:** Alghero (SS), Sardinia
- **Discovery:** 1903 by the Sella & Mosca winery during excavation for vineyards.
- **Structure:** 38 hypogean tombs (Domus de Janas) carved into a soft sandstone outcrop. 
- **URL Evidence:** https://www.sardegnacultura.it/

## 2. Archaeological Realities
- Tombs feature architectural details imitating real houses: steps, false doors, pillars, and carved bull heads (taurine protomes).
- Burials contained red ochre, used in funerary rites to symbolize blood and rebirth.

## 3. Folklore (Domus de Janas)
- Traditional belief: "Fairy houses" or "witch houses."
- Characteristics: Often described as beautiful women who weave on golden looms, but can turn vindictive if disrespected.
