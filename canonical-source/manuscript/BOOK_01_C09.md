# Book I Chapter 9: The Macomer Junction

The narrow-gauge train rattled through the interior, a metal snake winding through sun-scorched valleys and ancient basalt flows. Inside the dusty carriage, the tension was suffocating. Geronimo's hands throbbed in time with the clacking wheels, the bandages stark white against the worn leather of his jacket.

Katia kept her eyes fixed on the horizon, watching the terrain shift from coastal limestone to the harsh volcanic rock of the Marghine plateau. Macomer. The geographic and railway heart of Sardinia.

"We have thirty minutes before the connection to the main line," Maris murmured, her map spread across her knees. "The Sentinels know we're on the move, but they don't know our vector. If we stay on the train to Cagliari, we walk into a bottleneck."

"We get off at Macomer," André decided. "We go off-grid. Deep into the Barbagia."

As the train screeched into the Macomer junction, Geronimo felt a sudden, sharp heat against his chest. The Obsidian Eye, dormant since the necropolis, was reacting. Not to magic, but to the sheer magnetic density of the basalt plateau beneath them. It was pulling them East, toward the lawless mountains.
