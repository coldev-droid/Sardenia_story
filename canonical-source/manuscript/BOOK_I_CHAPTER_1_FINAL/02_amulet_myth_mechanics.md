## 1. Mythos & Origins

### The Myth of the Unlit Core
Long before the current epoch, ancient texts record the *Great Quenching*—a cosmic event where a primordial hearth-god was severed from its fire and cast into an endless glacial trench. The heat of its dying consciousness calcified into black, glassy slag: **Obsidian of the First Frost**. 

It is believed that these shards do not possess their own warmth; instead, they exist in a perpetual state of thermal vacuum. They are "hungry glass"—fragments of an ancient mind trying to reconstruct its lost fire by consuming the heat and memories of living beings.

> *"To hold the Splinter is to lend your soul’s fire to an ancient debt. When the warmth drains from your skin, your name is the first thing to freeze."*  
> — *Fragment from the Codex of Frozen Ember*

## 2. Physical Properties: The Obsidian Splinter

```
 [   THE OBSIDIAN SPLINTER   ]
  
          /\
         /  \     <- Jagged, non-euclidean fracture line
        / /\ \    <- Internal crimson striations (Veins of Stolen Heat)
       / /  \ \
      / /____\ \  <- Rimed edge; perpetually freezes ambient moisture
     /_/______\_\
          ||      <- Wrapped in silver-thread and blackened sinew
```

* **Visual Profile:** A jagged, wedge-shaped shard roughly four inches long. It appears blacker than natural glass, absorbing light without reflection. Deep within its core, faint red striations—resembling arterial networks—flicker only when heat is actively being siphoned.
* **Tactile Experience:** Drops the local air temperature within a 3-foot radius by 15–20°F. Touch induces an immediate pinprick sensation of extreme frostbite, followed by numbness.
* **Weight & Anomaly:** Unnaturally dense. It shifts weight subtly depending on the "charge" of stored thermal energy it holds.

## 3. The Thermal Amnesia Phenomenon

### Thermodynamic Memory Binding
In this world’s metaphysics, **cognition and identity are bound to thermal vitality**. The brain’s electrical pulses and emotional warmth are treated as a literal form of heat (*Therma*). 

When the Obsidian Splinter draws heat from a host, it does not merely drop somatic temperature; it leaches the **thermic energy holding neural pathways together**. Cold preserves the body, but it freezes and shatters memory.

```

|   Pure, unfeeling         Identity/Names vanish  Mid-term recall |
```

### Stages of Thermal Amnesia

#### Phase I: The Riming Edge (Somatic Cold)
* **Body Temperature:** 95.0°F – 91.0°F (35.0°C – 32.8°C)
* **Sensory Effects:** Breath turns to visible steam despite mild ambient weather. Shivering, pale lips, reduced tactile sensitivity in extremities.
* **Cognitive Effects:** Loss of immediate short-term memory (e.g., *Why did I walk into this room? What was I just holding?*).

#### Phase II: The Shivering Fog (Cognitive Decay)
* **Body Temperature:** 90.9°F – 84.0°F (32.7°C – 28.8°C)
* **Sensory Effects:** Frost forms on lashes, fingernails turn blue, heart rate drops below 40 BPM.
* **Cognitive Effects:** Medium-term memory erosion. Tactical knowledge remains intact (muscle memory, swordplay, spells), but personal contexts vanish (e.g., *I know how to fight with this knife, but I don't know who taught me or why I hold it*). Emotional bonds feel distant, like stories read about a stranger.

#### Phase III: Core Glaciation (Absolute Amnesia)
* **Body Temperature:** Below 84.0°F (28.8°C)
* **Sensory Effects:** Skin turns alabaster-pale and cold to the touch; no shivering (torpor stage). Pupil dilation.
* **Cognitive Effects:** Complete erasure of identity, names, and personal history. The individual operates on pure instinct and hyper-focused "Cold Clarity." Pain is ignored, emotion is nonexistent, and decision-making becomes ruthlessly logical.

## 4. Chapter 1 Narrative Mechanics

### Mechanical Integration

| Metric | System Trigger | Narrative Consequence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Thermal Gauge** | High / Normal | Full identity intact. Artifact is dormant; protagonist feels its dull, icy pulse against their chest. |
| **Thermal Bleed (Minor)** | Cold environment or prolonged contact | Short-term memory gaps. Protagonist forgets recent conversations, loses track of targets, experiences phantom cold. |
| **Thermal Bleed (Major)** | Active channeling or damage overload | Protagonist forgets key allies, personal goals, or their own name. Gains temporary immunity to fear/pain ("Cold Clarity"). |
| **Thawing (Recoil)** | Exposure to intense heat sources | Memories flood back broken, out of sequence, or permanently scorched (lost entirely). Severe nausea and psychological trauma. |

### The Thaw Penalty
Memories frozen by the Splinter do not return cleanly when warmed. 
* **Scorched Memory:** If warmed too rapidly (e.g., via open fire or direct flame magic), the memory is permanently lost, leaving a literal blank patch in the host's mind.
* **Fractured Thaw:** Gradual warming causes memories to return in distorted, terrifying fragments—mixing past events with nightmares.

## 5. Key Artifact Interactions (Chapter 1 Focus)

1. **The Binding Chain:** The Splinter must be encased in braided silver thread and shadow-sinew to prevent continuous thermal bleeding into the wearer. If the binding frays, the amnesia effect begins immediately.
2. **Sensory Anchors:** To counteract the thermal amnesia, the wearer must use physical "heat anchors"—burning sensory experiences (e.g., biting down on bitter salt, pressing a heated coin to the wrist, or repeating a spoken mantra) to lock memories in place before they freeze over.
3. **The Mirror Effect:** Looking into a reflective surface while under Phase II or III reveals no reflection of the wearer's breath or eyes—only the glowing crimson veins within the Obsidian Splinter resting against their skin.
