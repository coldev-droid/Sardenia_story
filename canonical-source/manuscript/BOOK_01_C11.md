# Book I Chapter 11: The Giants of Mont'e Prama

The ambush happened at dawn. 

They were navigating a dry riverbed near the Sinis peninsula, drawn by the Eye's resonance toward the site of the ancient stone giants. The Sentinel strike team didn't use helicopters—they were too smart for that. They used silent, electric off-road drones and high-velocity suppressed rifles.

The first shot shattered the Fiat's windshield, missing André's head by inches. 

"Contact! Three o'clock!" Maris roared, diving from the rolling vehicle. 

It wasn't a fight they could win with firepower. It was a fight for survival. Katia used the harsh, jagged terrain to their advantage, leading the team into a narrow sandstone slot canyon. The Sentinels followed, their tactical gear slowing them down in the tight, brutal squeeze.

Geronimo realized the canyon was unstable. He looked at the Obsidian Eye, then at the sheer rock wall above them. It wasn't magic—it was physics. He wedged the heavy, unyielding stone of the Eye into a critical stress fracture in the sandstone, using it as a lever. André threw his massive shoulder into it. 

The rock groaned, then collapsed, sealing the canyon in a massive avalanche of dust and stone, burying the Sentinels' path and buying them their lives.
