# Book I Chapter 12: The Threshold of Book II

They stood on a high ridge overlooking the Mediterranean, coated in sandstone dust and exhausted beyond measure. The Sentinel team was blocked, but the pursuit would only escalate.

Geronimo held the Obsidian Eye. It was scratched, the flawless surface marred by its use as a physical tool in the canyon, but the internal crimson light was stronger than ever. It was projecting a single, distinct beam of refracted sunlight across the water, pointing south. Toward the deep desert. Toward Africa.

"The Sardinian node is closed," Veerle said, reading the encrypted intercepts on her cracked tablet. "They're mobilizing a regional lockdown."

"Then we leave," Katia said, her jaw set. "We find a smuggler's boat. We follow the light."

Geronimo looked at his bandaged hands, feeling the permanent scars forming beneath the gauze. The cost of their survival. He closed his fingers around the Eye, the stone warm against his skin. The threshold was crossed. There was no going back to the academy, no going back to their old lives. The real journey was just beginning.
