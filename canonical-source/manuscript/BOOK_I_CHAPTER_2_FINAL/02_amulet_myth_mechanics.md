## 1. Mythological Foundation: The Amulet of the First Trench

### 1.1 Origin & Prophecy
In the pre-cataclysmic texts of the *Vael-Silt Scholars*, the relic known as the **Amulet of the First Trench** (or *The Viridian Eye*) was never intended to be worn by mortal hands. It was forged as a tuning fork—a focal anchor designed to bind the primal abyssal forces beneath the continental shelf. 

Ancient mariner myth speaks of the *Age of Still Waters*, during which the ocean floor remained dormant under a binding seal. The prophecy engraved upon the brass-and-marrow casing of the amulet reads:

> *"When the brass heart drinks the blood of the land,  
> The blue shall rot into the shade of newborn moss.  
> The Deep Slumberers shall open their blind eyes,  
> And the Cogas will claim the drowned sky."*

### 1.2 Arcane Functionality
The Amulet acts as an aetheric transducer. It extracts dormant subterranean mana from the sea floor and converts it into a high-density, low-frequency pulse. This pulse does not merely radiate through air; it saturates water molecules, destabilizing elemental bonds and forcing an instant transmutation of the sea’s chemical and magical properties.

## 2. Environmental Event: The Emerald Sea

When the Amulet is unsealed or brought to a critical threshold of attunement during Chapter 2, it triggers the **Emerald Shift**—a planetary environmental anomaly that transforms the surrounding ocean.

```
       │                                     │
       ▼                                     ▼
[Tectonic Trench Fracture] ────────► [Cogas Awakening Signal]
```

### 2.1 Visual & Sensory Manifestation
* **Color Shift:** Within minutes, the deep indigo ocean rots into an opaque, bioluminescent **emerald green**. The light source is not superficial; the water column glows internally, casting sickening green highlights onto ship hulls, coastal cliffs, and clouds.
* **Viscosity & Density:** The sea thickens. Waves no longer break with sharp white spray; instead, they roll like heavy liquid glass, creating a muffled, rhythmic thudding against shorelines.
* **Atmospheric Effect:** A warm, sulfurous vapor rises off the green tides. The sky directly above the affected area takes on a bruised violet tint, creating a stark, unnatural contrast with the vivid green waters below.
* **Acoustic Deadening:** Sound travels abnormally through the emerald water. Sub-surface screams or impacts can be heard crisp and clear miles away, while surface acoustics are dampened by a perpetual hum oscillating at **14 Hz**.

### 2.2 Magical & Ecological Properties
* **Chloric Aether Saturation:** The water becomes intensely conductive to arcane energy. Spells cast near or within the emerald sea receive increased potency but suffer unpredictable wild-magic surges.
* **Ecosystem Collapse:** Mundane sea life dies instantly or undergoes rapid, grotesque mutations. Shoals of fish float to the surface, eyes glassy and skin infused with glowing green veins, before dissolving into nutrient sludge that feeds the Cogas.

## 3. Entity Profile: The Cogas

The **Cogas** (singular: *Cogas*) are not merely beasts; they are colossal, biomechanical titan-gods forged in the oceanic trenches during the world's dawn. They have lain dormant for millennia, encased in calcified coral armor beneath miles of sediment, waiting for the Amulet's frequency to awaken them.

### 3.1 Anatomy & Morphology
* **Scale:** A single Cogas ranges from 150 to 400 meters in length, resembling a terrifying synthesis of a subterranean leviathan, an armored crustacean, and an ancient temple monolith.
* **Carapace:** Composed of layered obsidian bone, fossilized coral, and petrified kelp fibers. Standard weaponry and low-tier magic shatter against its hide.
* **The Viridian Core:** Visible through gaps in their chest armor is an exposed, pulsing organ that glows with the exact emerald hue of the transformed ocean. This organ acts as their sensory and magical nexus.
* **Sensory Systems:** The Cogas are blind to light. They perceive the world purely through **Aetheric Resonance** and **Vibrational Displacement**—meaning they track targets based on magic usage, heartbeats, and hull vibrations in the water.

### 3.2 The Awakening Sequence (Chapter 2 Timeline)

The awakening of the Cogas is not instantaneous; it progresses through four distinct narrative and mechanical phases as the Amulet warms up:

## 4. Mechanics & System Rules (Gameplay / Systems Integration)

### 4.1 Amulet Resonance Gauge (`Resonance_Val`)
In Chapter 2, the protagonist/party must manage or survive the Amulet's growing activation gauge (`0` to `100`).

* **0% - 24%:** Amulet is inert. Sea is normal blue.
* **25% - 49%:** Amulet glows faint green. Sea around the vessel turns pale emerald. Minor visual distortions.
* **50% - 74% (The Shift Threshold):** The entire sea turns deep emerald. Acoustic hum activates. Stealth underwater becomes impossible due to bioluminescence.
* **75% - 99%:** Cogas shadows become visible beneath the vessel hull. Sub-surface shockwaves deal periodic structural damage to ships.
* **100%:** Cogas Breach Event triggered. Boss encounter / Survival sequence begins.

### 4.2 Environmental Hazard Rules: The Emerald Water

Characters who fall into or operate within the Emerald Sea suffer specific mechanical effects:

1. **Viridian Sickness (Miasma/Toxin):**
   * Any non-aquatic creature submerged in emerald water must pass a **DC 15 Constitution Saving Throw** every round or gain 1 stack of *Viridian Sickness*.
   * *Effect:* Reduces maximum HP by 10% per stack and inflicts a glowing green outline visible through solid walls/water.
2. **Viscous Drag:**
   * Movement speed in water is halved (standard swimming penalty doubles). Rowing or mechanical ship propulsion operates at **50% efficiency**.
3. **Arcane Conduction:**
   * Any spell cast while standing in or touching the water has its range doubled, but the caster takes **1d6 Arcane Backlash damage per spell level** as raw energy surges through the water.

### 4.3 Cogas Detection & Aggro Mechanics

The Cogas do not attack randomly; they follow strict AI/Lore rules based on sensory input:

* **Sound/Vibration Aggro:** Engines, cannon fire, or heavy underwater impacts instantly draw a Cogas's attention within a 5-mile radius.
* **Arcane Flare Aggro:** Casting spells of Tier 3/Level 3 or higher creates an "Arcane Flare" in the emerald water, causing the nearest Cogas to immediately execute a *Breach Strike* targeting that location.
* **Amulet Masking:** The Amulet itself can be inverted or dampened using specific ancient rituals (found during Chapter 2 questlines) to temporarily render the vessel invisible to Cogas sensory tendrils for `300 seconds`.

## 5. Narrative Hooks & Chapter 2 Integration Points

1. **The Catalyst Event:** Chapter 2 opens with the Amulet reaching **50% Resonance** unexpectedly during a sea crossing or coastal battle. The dramatic visual shift of the blue ocean turning emerald green serves as the mid-chapter point-of-no-return.
2. **The Horror of the Ascent:** As the party navigates the thick green waters, massive ancient carapaces rise beneath their ship like moving islands, forcing players to navigate both navigation hazards and impending leviathan destruction.
3. **The Choice of the Core:** Players are faced with a central dilemma:
   * **Sustain the Signal:** Use the Amulet's power to defeat enemy fleets, accelerating the Cogas' awakening and environmental decay.
   * **Sever the Link:** Attempt a dangerous ritual to quench the Amulet, risking stranding the ship in a sea turned to sludge while dodging half-awakened, thrashing Cogas leviathans.
