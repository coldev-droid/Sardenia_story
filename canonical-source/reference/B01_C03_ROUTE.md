# BOOK I: THE OBSIDIAN EYE
## CHAPTER 3: ROUTE & SWARM CONTRACT

### 1. LOGISTICS & ROUTE
* **Unit ID:** B01_C03
* **Date/Time:** Late October. Night into early morning.
* **Origin:** The Grimaldi Ferry on the Mediterranean Sea.
* **Destination:** Porto Torres, Sardinia (Landfall).
* **Weather:** A savage Mediterranean squall that slowly breaks into a bruised, violent dawn as they reach the island.
* **Transport Reality:** The claustrophobia of a massive, swaying passenger ferry. The industrial thrum of engines, the sterile corridors, the vomit-stained carpets of seasick passengers. Then, the logistical reality of disembarking into a foreign port with dogs, no sleep, and hunters on their trail.

### 2. SARDINIAN ELEMENT & DISCOVERY
* **The Amulet's Vision & The Island's Welcome:** As the ferry nears the coast, the Obsidian Eye reacts to its homeland. The island is introduced not as a tourist destination, but as an ancient, sleeping titan. The scent of wild myrtle (*mirto*) and salt spray cuts through the diesel fumes. The jagged cliffs of Asinara and the Nuragic ruins in the distance establish a landscape built for myth, blood, and survival. 

### 3. ACTION & ADRENALINE ENGINE
* **Engine:** Claustrophobic paranoia and threshold crossing.
* **Objective:** Survive the ferry crossing and make landfall undetected.
* **The Turn:** The Hounds did not make it aboard in physical form, but their influence did. A storm-induced blackout on the ferry turns the lower passenger decks into a labyrinth. The magic is subtle and horrifying—reflections in the ferry's mirrors that don't match, a passenger who knows Geronimo's true name and tries to open the outer doors during the gale.
* **The Climax:** Escaping the crippled ferry in Porto Torres and the first steps onto Sardinian soil, realizing the island itself is a living battleground.

### 4. CHARACTER & EMOTIONAL CONSEQUENCE
* **Cast:** Geronimo, Katia, Maris, Veerle, Inga, André, Mia, Tina.
* **Geronimo:** The realization that he is the epicenter of the danger. He is no longer fleeing his past; he is bringing war to his ancestral home.
* **The Group:** Sleep deprivation fractures their patience. André's skepticism shatters on the ferry when he witnesses the mirror anomaly. They transition from "friends helping a friend" to a hardened, unified cell of survivors.

### 5. SWARM & AUDITOR MANDATE (APPROVED)
* **Word Count Target:** 5,200 - 6,000 words. (Strict enforcement).
* **Tone:** Epic, mythic, and cinematic (McKenzie style). Unrelenting tension. No filler.
* **Style:** High-fantasy gravity anchored in brutal modern reality. Magic has a cost. Every action has a bleeding consequence. 
* **The Mandate:** This is a promotional masterpiece for Sardinia, but forged in adrenaline and danger. The island must feel ancient, majestic, and inherently dangerous.
