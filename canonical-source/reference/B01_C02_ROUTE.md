# BOOK I: THE OBSIDIAN EYE
## CHAPTER 2: ROUTE & SWARM CONTRACT

### 1. LOGISTICS & ROUTE
* **Unit ID:** B01_C02
* **Date/Time:** Late October. Nighttime, continuing directly from C01.
* **Origin:** El Born district, Barcelona (Maris's apartment).
* **Destination:** The Port of Barcelona (Terminal Grimaldi), en route to Porto Torres, Sardinia.
* **Weather:** Torrential rain, violent squalls.
* **Transport Reality:** Navigating tight, flooded gothic streets on foot; booking emergency cargo/passenger ferry passage; the reality of port logistics, passport checks, and physical exhaustion.

### 2. SARDINIAN ELEMENT & DISCOVERY
* **The Amulet's Vision:** The Obsidian Eye projects a second fragmented memory—the extraction of *Byssus* (sea silk) under a moonlit Sardinian tide. It establishes the island not as a map to follow, but as a living, ancient entity tied to the sea. The ferry crossing itself becomes the physical threshold separating the mundane world from the labyrinth.

### 3. ACTION & ADRENALINE ENGINE
* **Engine:** Siege and Extraction.
* **Objective:** Escape the immediate supernatural threat converging on the apartment.
* **The Turn:** The pursuers do not attack with guns; they manipulate the environment. Rain hits the glass and moves *upward*. Shadows detach from the streetlamps. The "Hounds" of the Father operate on the periphery of logic.
* **The Climax:** A desperate sprint across the slippery rooftops of El Born and a tense infiltration into the ferry terminal as the gates close.

### 4. CHARACTER & EMOTIONAL CONSEQUENCE
* **Cast Assembled:** Geronimo, Katia, Maris, Veerle, Inga, André, plus Mia and Tina (the dogs).
* **Geronimo:** Must confess that his mother lied, and that he has brought the hunt to their door.
* **The Dogs (Mia & Tina):** They react first to the supernatural threat. Not with mindless barking, but with primal, intelligent dread. They ground the horror in reality.
* **The Consequence:** The group fractures initially out of disbelief, but bounds together out of survival. They cross the threshold. They are no longer safe in the normal world.

### 5. SWARM & AUDITOR MANDATE (APPROVED)
* **Tone:** High epic tension. No stalling. The exposition is delivered during physical movement.
* **Style:** Visceral, heavy, atmospheric. Every raindrop, every breath, every tactical decision matters.
* **Target:** ~4500-5200 words (Context window constrained; maximum possible dense generation authorized).
