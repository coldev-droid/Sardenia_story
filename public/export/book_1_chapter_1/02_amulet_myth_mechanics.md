# MECHANICS & RESONANCE RULES: CHAPTER 1

## Amulet Proximity Field: "The Obsidian Splinter"
*   **The Object:** Geronimo does not possess the Obsidian Eye. He possesses an unidentified obsidian splinter. The true Eye remains buried beneath Monte d'Accoddi.
*   **Mechanism (Thermal Memory Erasure):** The splinter does not merely radiate cold. It erases the *history* of heat. When the proximity field intensifies, it deletes thermal memory from the environment and the mind (e.g., a freshly struck match freezes; the memory of a warm room becomes icy). 
*   **Proximity Inversion:** As the ferry approaches Sardinia, the true Eye beneath the ziggurat uses the splinter not as a beacon, but as a lens. 

## Mythic Manifestation Law: "The Identity Theft & Counting Inversion"
*   **Cogas Manifestation:** The Cogas do not physically fly across the sea as giant insects. They manifest through memory and identity replacement. They infect the passengers, replacing them one remembered feature at a time (eye color, jawline, voice) until the original human is gone.
*   **The Sickle's Failure:** Katia uses a Villacidro toothless sickle, relying on the traditional 1-to-7 counting loop to paralyze the Cogas. However, the true Eye's proximity *inverts* this law. Instead of freezing the Coga, every number counted grants the shifting entity more solid identity and mass. Counting them empowers them.
*   **The Jana Collision:** A Jana (Sardinian weaver of fate) is trapped or residing within the intricate weave of the ferry's heavy maritime safety ropes. When the sickle fails, the Jana actively interferes, shifting the physical geometry of the rope knots to tangle the Coga's perception, creating a genuine myth-on-myth collision.

## The Torre Aragonese Geometry (Survival Mechanic)
*   The Cogas' mythological limitation is locked to base-7 logic (the 1-to-7 counting loop). 
*   Upon arrival, the architectural memory of Roman Turris Libisonis overlaps modern Molo Ponente. The Torre Aragonese casts a stark shadow. Because the tower is explicitly *octagonal* (8-sided), its geometric shadow exists outside the Cogas' base-7 comprehension. The 8th angle creates a blind spot where Geronimo and Katia can hide.
